About This Treatment

CO2 Fractional Laser Overview

Deep acne scars, surgical scars, fine lines, wrinkles, and stretch marks can significantly impact your skin's texture and your self-confidence. The CO₂ Fractional Laser at Relief Skin Clinic is one of the most powerful skin resurfacing technologies available, delivering remarkable results for textural concerns that creams alone cannot address.

Unlike older ablative lasers that treated 100% of the skin surface, fractional CO₂ technology creates thousands of microscopic treatment zones (called microthermal zones) while leaving the surrounding tissue intact. This fractional approach triggers a powerful wound-healing response — your body produces fresh collagen and elastin fibres that literally rebuild the skin from within.

The result is smoother, firmer, and more even-textured skin with significantly reduced scarring. Each session progressively remodels the deep dermal layers, making CO₂ Fractional Laser the gold standard for deep scar revision and skin rejuvenation.

Step-by-Step Process

The CO2 Fractional Laser Procedure

Here is exactly what to expect during your treatment session at our clinic.

01
Consultation & Assessment

Dr. Bhimani evaluates the type and depth of scarring using clinical examination and dermoscopy. A treatment plan with the appropriate settings is created.

02
Numbing & Preparation

A topical anaesthetic cream is applied 45–60 minutes before the procedure to ensure comfort. The area is cleansed and the cream is removed.

03
Fractional Laser Treatment

The CO₂ laser handpiece is carefully passed over the treatment area. You may feel a warm, prickling sensation. The procedure takes 45–60 minutes depending on the area size.

04
Post-Treatment Recovery

Redness and swelling are expected for 3–5 days. A healing ointment is applied. Peeling occurs around days 5–7 as new skin emerges. Complete healing takes 10–14 days.

What to Expect

Expected Results & Timeline

Results vary by individual, but here is a typical timeline of improvement.

Week 1

Skin appears red and swollen initially. Peeling begins around day 5 as the treated micro-zones heal with new, fresh skin.

Week 2–4

Redness gradually fades. New skin is visibly smoother and tighter. Early collagen remodelling begins.

Month 1–3

Significant improvement in scar depth and skin texture. Pores appear smaller. Fine lines are reduced.

Month 3–6

Collagen remodelling continues. Final results emerge with firmer, smoother, and more youthful skin. Deep scars are substantially flattened.
