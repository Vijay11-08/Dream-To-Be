About This Treatment

Hydra Facial & Rejuvenation Overview

The HydraFacial is the most popular non-invasive skin rejuvenation treatment in the world — and for good reason. This multi-step facial combines cleansing, exfoliation, extraction, hydration, and antioxidant protection in a single session, delivering immediate visible results with zero downtime.

At Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic, our HydraFacial protocol uses a patented vortex-fusion technology that simultaneously dislodges impurities from pores while infusing the skin with intensive moisturising serums, hyaluronic acid, peptides, and antioxidants. The result is deeply cleansed, intensely hydrated, and visibly glowing skin — all within a single appointment.

Unlike traditional facials that can leave skin irritated, the HydraFacial is gentle enough for even sensitive and acne-prone skin. It's the perfect treatment before a special event, or as a monthly maintenance ritual for consistently beautiful skin.

Step-by-Step Process

The Hydra Facial & Rejuvenation Procedure

Here is exactly what to expect during your treatment session at our clinic.

01
Cleansing & Exfoliation

Gentle resurfacing removes dead skin cells and reveals a fresh layer of skin. A mild acid solution loosens debris and sebum from pores.

02
Acid Peel (Gentle)

A blend of glycolic and salicylic acids is applied briefly to soften impurities lodged in pores — without the irritation of a traditional peel.

03
Painless Extractions

The HydraFacial's vortex suction tip painlessly extracts blackheads and impurities. You can actually see the extracted debris in the collection chamber.

04
Hydration & Protection

Intensive serums containing hyaluronic acid, peptides, and antioxidants are infused into the skin. LED light therapy may be added for enhanced results.
