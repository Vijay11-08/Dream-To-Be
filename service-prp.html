About This Treatment

PRP Treatment & Mesotherapy Overview

PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy is a revolutionary regenerative treatment that uses your body's own healing mechanisms to combat hair loss and rejuvenate skin. At Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic, we use advanced double-spin centrifuge technology to concentrate the growth factors in your blood to levels 5–8 times higher than normal.

A small sample of your blood is drawn and processed in a centrifuge to separate the platelet-rich plasma from other blood components. This concentrated plasma — packed with growth factors like PDGF, TGF-β, VEGF, and EGF — is then injected into the scalp or skin, where it stimulates cellular regeneration, hair follicle revival, and collagen production.

Mesotherapy complements PRP by delivering a customised cocktail of vitamins, amino acids, minerals, and hyaluronic acid directly into the mesoderm (middle layer of skin). When combined, PRP and Mesotherapy offer a powerful, natural approach to hair restoration and skin rejuvenation.

Why Choose This Treatment

Benefits of PRP Treatment & Mesotherapy

Discover why thousands of patients trust Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic for their prp treatment & mesotherapy needs.

100% Natural — Your Own Blood

Since PRP uses your own platelet-rich plasma, there is virtually zero risk of allergic reaction, rejection, or side effects.

Stimulates Hair Growth

Growth factors activate dormant hair follicles, increase follicle size, and prolong the active growth phase (anagen) of the hair cycle.

Strengthens Existing Hair

PRP doesn't just regrow hair — it thickens and strengthens existing hair, reducing breakage and improving hair quality.

Skin Rejuvenation

When used on the face, PRP stimulates collagen production, improving skin texture, reducing fine lines, and diminishing dark circles.

Minimal Downtime

Most patients experience mild scalp tenderness for a few hours. There are no bandages, no stitches, and no activity restrictions.

Synergy with Mesotherapy

Combining PRP with vitamin-enriched mesotherapy cocktails amplifies results — the growth factors from PRP work alongside essential nutrients to maximise regeneration.

Step-by-Step Process

The PRP Treatment & Mesotherapy Procedure

Here is exactly what to expect during your treatment session at our clinic.

01
Blood Collection

A small amount of blood (15–20 ml) is drawn from your arm — similar to a routine blood test. This is processed in a sterile centrifuge.

02
PRP Preparation

The blood is centrifuged using a double-spin technique to isolate the platelet-rich plasma. This concentrated plasma contains 5–8x the normal growth factor levels.

03
Scalp/Skin Numbing

A topical anaesthetic cream or nerve block is applied to the treatment area to ensure complete comfort during injections.

04
Micro-Injections

The concentrated PRP (with or without mesotherapy cocktail) is injected into the scalp or facial skin using ultra-fine needles at precise depths.
