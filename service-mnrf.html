About This Treatment

MNRF (Microneedling RF) Overview

MNRF (Microneedling Radiofrequency) is a breakthrough treatment that combines the proven benefits of microneedling with the deep collagen-stimulating power of radiofrequency energy. At Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic, we use this technology to treat acne scars, wrinkles, and skin laxity with remarkable results — particularly suited for Indian skin tones.

The device uses gold-plated insulated microneedles that penetrate the skin to a precisely controlled depth. Once at the target depth, radiofrequency energy is released only at the needle tip — not at the skin surface. This crucial design feature means the thermal energy is delivered exactly where collagen remodelling is needed (the deep dermis) while the surface epidermis is protected.

This makes MNRF significantly safer for darker skin tones than traditional fractional lasers, as the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation is dramatically reduced. The combination of controlled micro-injuries and deep RF heating triggers an intense collagen and elastin remodelling cascade, resulting in smoother, tighter, and more youthful skin.

Why Choose This Treatment

Benefits of MNRF (Microneedling RF)

Discover why thousands of patients trust Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic for their mnrf (microneedling rf) needs.

Safer for Indian Skin

Because RF energy is delivered at the needle tip (not the surface), the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation is much lower than with fractional lasers.

Deep Collagen Stimulation

RF energy at controlled depths triggers robust collagen and elastin production in the deep dermis — where structural skin support matters most.

Scar Remodelling

MNRF breaks down old scar tissue and replaces it with fresh collagen fibres, progressively smoothing out even deep acne scars.

Skin Tightening

The thermal effect of RF energy causes immediate collagen contraction, providing a visible tightening effect that continues to improve over months.

Pore Size Reduction

The combination of microneedling and RF shrinks enlarged pores, giving the skin a more refined and smooth appearance.

Shorter Recovery

Compared to ablative CO₂ laser, MNRF has a shorter recovery period of just 2–4 days, with only mild redness and swelling.

Step-by-Step Process

The MNRF (Microneedling RF) Procedure

Here is exactly what to expect during your treatment session at our clinic.

01
Skin Assessment

Dr. Bhimani evaluates your scar type, skin thickness, and condition to determine the optimal needle depth and RF energy settings.

02
Numbing

A topical anaesthetic cream is applied 45–60 minutes before the procedure. This ensures complete comfort during treatment.

03
MNRF Treatment

The handpiece is stamped across the treatment area in a systematic grid pattern. You may feel a mild pricking and warm sensation. The session takes 45–60 minutes.

04
Post-Treatment Care

Mild redness and pin-point bleeding are normal and resolve within 24–48 hours. A healing serum is applied. Sunscreen is mandatory.

What to Expect

Expected Results & Timeline

Results vary by individual, but here is a typical timeline of improvement.

Day 1–3

Mild redness and micro-swelling — similar to a mild sunburn. Pin-point marks fade quickly.

Week 1–2

Redness resolves. Skin begins to feel smoother as the initial healing response kicks in.

Month 1–2

Early collagen remodelling is visible. Scars appear shallower. Pores look smaller. Skin feels firmer.

Month 3–6

Full collagen remodelling results are visible. Significant improvement in scar depth, skin texture, and overall tightness.
