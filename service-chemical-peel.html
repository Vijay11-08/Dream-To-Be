About This Treatment

Chemical Peel & IPL Laser Overview

Chemical Peels and IPL (Intense Pulsed Light) Laser are two of the most versatile and effective treatments in modern dermatology. At Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic, we combine these modalities — or use them independently — to address a wide range of skin concerns including active acne, acne marks, pigmentation, melasma, and dull skin.

Our medical-grade chemical peels use carefully selected acids (glycolic, salicylic, lactic, TCA, or combination peels) at precise concentrations to exfoliate damaged surface skin cells. This controlled exfoliation accelerates cell turnover, unclogs pores, reduces acne, and reveals fresher, brighter skin underneath.

The IPL Laser complements peels by delivering broad-spectrum light that targets pigment, redness, and broken capillaries beneath the skin surface. Together, these treatments offer a comprehensive approach to skin rejuvenation — improving both texture and tone simultaneously.

Benefits of Chemical Peel & IPL Laser

Discover why thousands of patients trust Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic for their chemical peel & ipl laser needs.

Immediate Glow

Even after a single peel session, most patients notice brighter, smoother skin. The exfoliation removes the dull, dead cell layer instantly.

Acne Control

Salicylic acid peels penetrate into pores, dissolving the oil and debris that cause breakouts. Regular peels significantly reduce acne frequency.

Pigmentation Reduction

IPL targets melanin clusters while peels accelerate cell turnover — a dual attack on stubborn pigmentation and melasma.

Superficial peels (glycolic, lactic) require no downtime. You can resume your routine immediately with a natural glow.

Collagen Stimulation

Medium-depth peels (TCA) trigger a healing response that boosts collagen production, improving skin firmness and reducing fine lines.

Customisable Protocols

Our dermatologist selects the peel type, concentration, and pH based on your specific skin concern, type, and sensitivity — ensuring safety and maximum efficacy.

Step-by-Step Process

The Chemical Peel & IPL Laser Procedure

Here is exactly what to expect during your treatment session at our clinic.

01
Skin Analysis

Dr. Bhimani or Dr. Kanani examines your skin to determine the appropriate peel type and strength. Pre-peel preparation may include using specific products for 1–2 weeks.

02
Cleansing & Application

The skin is thoroughly cleansed and degreased. The chemical solution is applied in controlled layers. You may feel a mild tingling or warming sensation.

03
Neutralisation

After the prescribed time (2–10 minutes depending on peel depth), the peel is neutralised and removed. A soothing serum and sunscreen are applied.

04
Post-Peel Care

Mild redness and flaking may occur for 2–5 days with medium peels. Strict sun protection (SPF 50+) is essential. Moisturising helps the peeling process.
