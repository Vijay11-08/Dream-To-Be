About This Treatment

RF Cautery Overview

RF (Radiofrequency) Cautery is a safe, precise, and virtually painless method for removing unwanted skin growths such as skin tags, warts, moles, milia, sebaceous cysts, and other small benign skin lesions. At Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic, we use advanced radiofrequency technology that produces controlled thermal energy to precisely remove or ablate targeted tissue.

Unlike surgical excision, RF Cautery does not require stitches and causes minimal damage to surrounding tissue. The radiofrequency energy cuts and coagulates simultaneously, meaning there is very little bleeding and the wound heals quickly with minimal scarring.

This is one of the most commonly requested procedures at our clinic. Many patients come in with multiple skin tags or warts that have been bothersome for years. In just one short appointment, these can be permanently removed, giving you smoother, cleaner skin.

Step-by-Step Process

The RF Cautery Procedure

Here is exactly what to expect during your treatment session at our clinic.

01
Consultation & Assessment

Dr. Bhimani examines the lesion to confirm it is benign. If there is any clinical concern, a biopsy may be recommended before removal.

02
Local Anaesthesia

A tiny amount of local anaesthetic is injected around the lesion. This numbs the area completely so you feel no pain during the procedure.

03
RF Ablation

The radiofrequency probe is used to precisely remove or ablate the lesion. The energy simultaneously cuts and cauterises, preventing bleeding.

04
Post-Procedure Care

A small wound remains that is covered with an antibiotic ointment. It heals within 5–10 days, leaving minimal to no scarring.

What to Expect

Expected Results & Timeline

Results vary by individual, but here is a typical timeline of improvement.

Immediately After

The lesion is completely removed. A small, superficial wound remains at the treatment site.

Days 3–5

A scab forms over the wound. This is part of the natural healing process.

Days 7–14

The scab falls off, revealing new pink skin underneath. This gradually blends with the surrounding skin tone.

Month 1–2

The treatment site is fully healed. Any residual pinkness fades completely, leaving smooth, clear skin.
