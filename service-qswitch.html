About This Treatment

Q-Switch Nd:YAG Laser Overview

Pigmentation, dark spots, melasma, and unwanted tattoos are among the most stubborn skin concerns. The Q-Switch Nd:YAG Laser at Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic delivers ultra-short, high-energy laser pulses measured in nanoseconds to shatter pigment particles beneath the skin — without damaging surrounding tissue.

This dual-wavelength laser (1064nm and 532nm) allows our dermatologist to treat a wide spectrum of pigmentary conditions. The longer wavelength penetrates deeper for conditions like melasma, dermal pigmentation, and dark tattoo inks, while the shorter wavelength targets superficial spots, freckles, and red/yellow tattoo inks.

The shattered pigment particles are then naturally absorbed and eliminated by your body's immune system over the following weeks, revealing clearer, more luminous skin with each session.

Step-by-Step Process

The Q-Switch Nd:YAG Laser Procedure

Here is exactly what to expect during your treatment session at our clinic.

01
Skin Analysis & Diagnosis

Dr. Bhimani uses dermoscopy to precisely identify the type and depth of pigmentation. This determines the wavelength and energy settings.

02
Preparation

The treatment area is cleansed. Protective eyewear is fitted. No anaesthesia is usually needed, but a numbing cream can be applied for sensitive areas.

03
Laser Treatment

The Q-Switch handpiece delivers rapid laser pulses to the pigmented areas. You may feel a mild snapping sensation, similar to a rubber band flick.

04
Post-Treatment

Mild redness or darkening of treated spots is normal and fades within a few days. Strict sunscreen use (SPF 50+) is mandatory between sessions.

What to Expect

Expected Results & Timeline

Results vary by individual, but here is a typical timeline of improvement.

Days 1–3

Treated pigmented spots may initially darken — this is a positive sign that the pigment has been targeted. Mild redness resolves.

After 3 Sessions

After 6 Sessions
