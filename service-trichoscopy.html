About This Treatment

Trichoscopy & Dermoscopy Overview

Trichoscopy (scalp & hair dermoscopy) and Dermoscopy (skin surface microscopy) are essential diagnostic tools used at Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic to examine hair and skin conditions at a magnification of up to 100x. These non-invasive techniques allow Dr. Sagar R. Bhimani to make precise diagnoses that are impossible with the naked eye.

In trichoscopy, a specialised dermatoscope is placed on the scalp to examine hair follicle density, hair shaft thickness, scalp health, and vascular patterns. This helps in accurately diagnosing the type and stage of hair loss — whether it is androgenetic alopecia, alopecia areata, telogen effluvium, or scarring alopecia — and guides the treatment plan.

In dermoscopy, the same principle is applied to skin lesions — moles, pigmented spots, and unusual growths are examined under magnification to identify patterns that differentiate benign lesions from potentially concerning ones. This is a critical tool for early detection and accurate clinical decision-making.

Benefits of Trichoscopy & Dermoscopy

Magnified visualisation reveals diagnostic clues invisible to the naked eye — hair shaft abnormalities, miniaturisation, broken hairs, and vascular patterns.

Completely Painless & Non-Invasive

You can see your own scalp and skin on the screen in real-time. This helps you understand your condition and builds confidence in your treatment plan.

Step-by-Step Process

The Trichoscopy & Dermoscopy Procedure

Here is exactly what to expect during your treatment session at our clinic.

01
Consultation

Dr. Bhimani discusses your concerns and medical history. The relevant areas for examination are identified.

02
Dermatoscope Examination

A handheld digital dermatoscope with a polarised LED light source is placed on the scalp or skin surface. High-resolution magnified images are captured.

03
Analysis & Diagnosis

The captured images are analysed on-screen. Hair follicle density, shaft diameter, and structural patterns are evaluated. For skin lesions, characteristic patterns are assessed.

04
Report & Treatment Plan

Findings are explained to you with visual references. A personalised treatment plan is created based on the diagnosis. Baseline images are saved for future comparison.
