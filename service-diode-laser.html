About This Treatment

Diode Laser Hair Reduction Overview

Unwanted body and facial hair can be a persistent concern affecting both men and women. At Relief Skin & Cosmetic Clinic, we use the latest-generation 810nm Diode Laser — the gold standard in permanent hair reduction — to deliver smooth, hair-free skin with minimal discomfort.

Unlike traditional methods like waxing, threading, or shaving that offer only temporary relief, Diode Laser targets the melanin pigment deep within the hair follicle. The laser energy is absorbed by the follicle, destroying its ability to regrow hair while leaving the surrounding skin completely unharmed.

Our clinic uses an advanced cooling system integrated into the handpiece, which keeps the skin comfortable throughout the procedure. This makes our treatments virtually painless — even on sensitive areas like the upper lip, underarms, and bikini line.

Benefits of Diode Laser Hair Reduction

After completing the recommended number of sessions, most patients experience up to 85–90% permanent hair reduction. Occasional maintenance sessions may be needed once a year.

Step-by-Step Process

The Diode Laser Hair Reduction Procedure

Here is exactly what to expect during your treatment session at our clinic.

01
Consultation & Skin Assessment

Our dermatologist examines your skin type, hair colour, and growth pattern. A customized treatment plan is created based on your unique requirements.

02
Preparation

The target area is cleansed, and a thin layer of cooling gel is applied. Protective eyewear is provided for both the patient and the clinician.

03
Laser Application

The Diode Laser handpiece is moved across the treatment area in a systematic pattern. The integrated cooling tip protects the skin while the laser energy penetrates to the follicle root.

04
Post-Treatment Care

Mild redness may appear, which subsides within 1–2 hours. A soothing cream is applied. You can resume normal activities immediately.

What to Expect

Expected Results & Timeline

Results vary by individual, but here is a typical timeline of improvement.

After 1st Session

You will notice that treated hairs begin to shed within 7–14 days. New growth in the treated area is noticeably finer and slower.

After 3–4 Sessions

Significant reduction in hair density — approximately 50–60%. The remaining hairs are much thinner and lighter in colour.

After 6–8 Sessions

85–90% permanent hair reduction is typically achieved. The skin feels smoother and more even-toned.

Maintenance

An annual touch-up session may be recommended to address any dormant follicles that become active due to hormonal changes.
